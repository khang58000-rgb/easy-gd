# Instant Jump

Jump from the **exact frame you click** - no waiting for the next frame.

## Features
- **Instant click** - presses and releases are handed to the game the moment they arrive.
- **Input queuing** - every tap lasts at least one physics step, so fast ship / UFO tapping and **Wave** spam always register and straight flying stays consistent.
- **Input buffering** - click a moment before landing and the jump fires on touchdown. Fully configurable.
- **Double-click fix** - ignores mouse / controller bounce.
- **Slope assist** - wider buffer window on and just after slopes.
- **Jump prediction** - the buffer window adapts to your fall speed.

Everything can be turned on or off, and tuned, in the mod settings.

## Notes
Some leaderboards and record rules treat input-assist mods as not allowed. Check the rules before submitting a record.
